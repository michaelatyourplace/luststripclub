The Grand Strand's adult nightlife scene is smaller than it used to be. The area once had well over a dozen gentlemen's clubs, but today the options come down to a handful of established venues split between two pockets: the Seaboard Street and Mr. Joe White Avenue corridor in Myrtle Beach, and the Highway 17 South strip in Atlantic Beach and North Myrtle Beach.

This directory lists every club currently operating along the Grand Strand with its address, phone number, typical hours, and what sets it apart. If you're planning a bachelorette party and want male entertainment instead of a club night, skip ahead to our male strippers section for the area's top private-party pick.

Strip Clubs in Myrtle Beach

Masters Gentleman's Club

Myrtle Beach
  • 1901 Mr. Joe White Ave, Suite M, Myrtle Beach, SC 29577
  • (843) 916-0972
  • Daily, 1 PM – 2 AM

The largest and longest-running club in Myrtle Beach, with a big roster of entertainers every night, private VIP areas, valet parking, and a food menu. Contact their concierge ahead of time to ask about free admission and shuttle pickup.

The Treasure Club

Myrtle Beach
  • 716 Seaboard St, Myrtle Beach, SC 29577
  • (843) 945-2270
  • Mon–Thu 12 PM – 2 AM · Fri 12 PM – 3 AM · Sat–Sun 12 PM – 2 AM

Open since 2008 and known for a relaxed, sports-bar-meets-club atmosphere. The Treasure Club shows UFC fights and major pay-per-view events, and weekday afternoons draw a lunch crowd.

Lust Gentlemen's Club & Steakhouse

Myrtle Beach
  • 804 Seaboard St, Myrtle Beach, SC 29577
  • Call ahead for reservations
  • Daily from 11 AM, open late

The newest club on the Strand, opened in the former Derriere's building just down the street from the Treasure Club. Lust pairs a full bar with a steakhouse-style kitchen that serves from open to close, making it an easy dinner-and-a-show stop.

Strip Clubs in North Myrtle Beach & Atlantic Beach

Thee DollHouse

North Myrtle Beach
  • 3001 Hwy 17 S, North Myrtle Beach, SC 29582
  • (843) 272-3329
  • Daily, 4 PM – 4 AM · Happy hour 4 PM – 8 PM

A Grand Strand institution for nearly four decades and the most upscale-feeling room on the north end. Expect VIP packages, premium liquor and bottle service, and the latest closing time on the coast, which makes it the natural last stop of the night.

Visit Website

Pearls Cabaret / Platinum Drip

Atlantic Beach
  • 3106 Hwy 17 S, North Myrtle Beach (Atlantic Beach), SC 29582
  • (843) 273-0251
  • Late-night events, typically Fri & Sun from 9 PM

An urban-style club just up Highway 17 from Thee DollHouse, running its "Platinum Drip" party nights with DJs and a nightclub feel. It's more of an event-night venue than an every-night club, so check the schedule before you go.

Clubs You May Still See Online

Older listings still show a few names that are no longer operating on the Grand Strand. Derriere's on Seaboard Street is now Lust, and Tiffany's Cabaret and the Gold Club were shut down years ago. Strip Club Palace shows up in search results but is a limo and transportation service rather than a club. Hours and events change often, so always call ahead.

Tips for a Night Out on the Strand

  • Bring ID: Every club checks, and most with a full bar are 21+.
  • Carry cash: Dances and tips run on cash. ATMs are available but fees add up.
  • Call ahead: Hours shift with the season, bike weeks, and golf season. Confirm before you drive over.
  • Plan your ride: The Myrtle Beach clubs and the Highway 17 clubs are about 15–20 minutes apart. Use a rideshare or ask about club shuttles.
  • Ask about groups: Some clubs have policies for large parties or all-female groups, so let them know your headcount in advance.
  • Respect the rules: Follow each club's no-touch and no-photo policies. Staff enforce them.
  • Bachelorette party? For a private show where the bride is the star, book Grand Strand Gent for your rental instead.
