About the Guide

An independent, no-nonsense look at adult nightlife on the Grand Strand.

Who We Are

Grand Strand Club Guide is an independent directory of strip clubs and adult entertainment along the South Carolina coast. We cover the venues visitors actually ask about, from the clubs off Seaboard Street and Mr. Joe White Avenue in Myrtle Beach to the Highway 17 strip in Atlantic Beach and North Myrtle Beach.

Search results for this area are full of outdated listings, closed clubs, and businesses that aren't clubs at all. Our goal is simple: keep one clean, current list so golf groups, bachelor and bachelorette parties, and anyone visiting the beach can plan a night out without guesswork.

How We Build Our Listings

Every club in the directory is checked for a working address, phone number, and current hours. When a venue closes, changes names, or moves, we update or remove it and note the change so readers aren't sent to an empty building. Listings are free, and no club pays for placement or a better write-up.

Local Coverage

Myrtle Beach, North Myrtle Beach, Atlantic Beach, and the South Strand down to Pawleys Island.

Kept Current

Closed and renamed clubs are flagged so you know exactly what's open this season.

Independent

We're not owned by or affiliated with any club listed in the directory.
